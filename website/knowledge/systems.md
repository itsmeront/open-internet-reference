# Systems Under Open-Internet Pressure

Implementations OIR tracks for design, governance, and legal exposure — privacy-preserving networks, messaging systems, and related technology records. These are not a product directory; they are systems where censorship resistance, encryption, moderation, and state pressure become concrete.

Organization records (`ORG-*`) and technology records (`TECH-*`) both appear here when OIR documents the system as an open-internet pressure point.

## Anonymous and Censorship-Resistant Networks

- [`ORG-TOR-PROJECT`](../generated/source-docs/knowledge/organizations/ORG-TOR-PROJECT.md): **Tor Project** — nonprofit developing onion-routing tools for anonymous communication.
- [`TECH-HYPHANET`](../generated/source-docs/knowledge/technical/TECH-HYPHANET.md): **Hyphanet (formerly Freenet)** — censorship-resistant peer-to-peer publishing network; later added Web of Trust spam resistance after unmoderated forums were disrupted.
- [`TECH-GNUNET`](../generated/source-docs/knowledge/technical/TECH-GNUNET.md): **GNUnet** — Free Software stack for secure, decentralized, privacy-preserving networking, including namespace/trust responses to keyword spam.

## Secure Messaging

- [`ORG-SIGNAL`](../generated/source-docs/knowledge/organizations/ORG-SIGNAL.md): **Signal** — nonprofit-associated encrypted messaging service and protocol family.
- [`ORG-TELEGRAM`](../generated/source-docs/knowledge/organizations/ORG-TELEGRAM.md): **Telegram** — messaging platform documented in OIR for government pressure over moderation, encryption access, and alleged facilitation of unlawful activity.
- [`TECH-PGP`](../generated/source-docs/knowledge/technical/TECH-PGP.md): **Pretty Good Privacy (PGP)** — foundational civilian encryption software whose 1991 distribution led to a closed U.S. criminal export investigation.

## Privacy Tech Under Sanctions or Seizure Pressure

- [`TECH-GRAPHENEOS`](../generated/source-docs/knowledge/technical/TECH-GRAPHENEOS.md): **GrapheneOS** — privacy- and security-focused mobile OS documented in OIR for duress-wipe features raised in border-search / device-seizure proceedings.
- [`TECH-TORNADO-CASH`](../generated/source-docs/knowledge/technical/TECH-TORNADO-CASH.md): **Tornado Cash** — open-source Ethereum mixer protocol subject to OFAC designation litigation and developer criminal cases.
- [`TECH-SAMOURAI-WALLET`](../generated/source-docs/knowledge/technical/TECH-SAMOURAI-WALLET.md): **Samourai Wallet** — Bitcoin privacy wallet whose co-founders pled guilty in 2025 to conspiracy involving a money transmitting business that transmitted crime proceeds.
