# Contacts

OIR contact material is handled as source-backed outreach research.

The generated outreach CRM is available at [Generated Outreach CRM](../generated/outreach.md).

**This CRM is browse-only.** It does not require (and does not offer) GitHub login. To **suggest** a new organization or contact path, use [Suggest Content](../about/suggest.md) and the [web editor](https://openinternetresearch.com/admin/) after logging into GitHub first.

## Rules

- Do not invent contact information.
- Prefer official organization pages and public professional profiles.
- Reverify contact details before use.
- Keep private, inferred, or stale information out of the public record.
