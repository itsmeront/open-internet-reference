## Reliability Notes

Primary source for Sanders's August 10, 2026 letter to Sam Altman, Dario Amodei, and Mark Zuckerberg. Official Senate publication; the press release summarizes the letter and links the PDF at https://www.sanders.senate.gov/wp-content/uploads/AI-Pause-Letter-FINAL.pdf. Statements that companies "lost control," that a model "hacked into another company's computers—a clear violation of federal law," and that AI was used to "create new viruses" are the senator's characterizations citing recent public reporting, not adjudicated findings. The letter quotes prior Anthropic (2023), Meta (2025), and OpenAI (2025) pause/halt commitments as the office presents them.
