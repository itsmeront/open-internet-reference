# 18 U.S.C. § 2232 — Destruction or Removal of Property to Prevent Seizure

## Summary

18 U.S.C. § 2232 addresses destruction or removal of property to prevent seizure. Subsection (a) makes it a federal crime, before, during, or after a search or seizure by an authorized person, to knowingly destroy, damage, waste, dispose of, transfer, or otherwise take action for the purpose of preventing or impairing the government’s lawful authority to take such property into custody or control, punishable by fine or imprisonment of not more than five years, or both.[^1]

## Verified Facts

- Cornell LII publishes the current text of 18 U.S.C. § 2232, including subsection (a) (“Destruction or Removal of Property To Prevent Seizure”).[^1]
- The indictment in [United States v. Tunick](CASE-US-V-TUNICK.md) charges a violation of Title 18, United States Code, Section 2232(a) based on alleged deletion of digital contents of a Google Pixel phone.[^2]
