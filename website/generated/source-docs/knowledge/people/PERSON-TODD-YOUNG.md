# Todd Young

## Summary

Todd Young is a U.S. Senator from Indiana who served on the 2024 Bipartisan Senate AI Working Group, sits on the Senate AI Caucus, and whose office identifies artificial intelligence as a core legislative focus alongside the CHIPS and Science Act.

## Verified Facts

- Official Senate biography states that Young joined the Senate in 2017 after three House terms, served in the Marine Corps, and sits on Finance; Commerce, Science and Transportation; Small Business; and Intelligence.[^1]
- The same biography states that the CHIPS and Science Act, which it calls his landmark legislation, became law in 2022, and lists "harnessing the potential of artificial intelligence" among his Senate focuses.[^1]
- The May 15, 2024 Working Group announcement names Young as a member of the four-senator group that released the Senate AI roadmap.[^2]
- Heinrich's Senate AI Caucus page lists Young as a member.[^3]

## Historical Context

Young is the Republican Commerce-committee counterpart to Heinrich on research-resource and innovation bills. The Working Group roadmap's NAIRR / CREATE AI headings match that portfolio. This is not a pause-and-ban record.

## Relevance to Open Source and Software Companies

Commerce Committee AI bills on research clouds, voluntary standards, and international coordination directly affect who can train and evaluate models outside the largest labs. CHIPS-funded domestic compute also changes where open and commercial training can physically run.
