# Margot Kaminski

## Summary

Margot Kaminski is the Moses Lasky Professor of Law at the University of Colorado and Director of the Privacy Initiative at Silicon Flatirons, a scholar of AI law, data privacy, and the First Amendment who is co-authoring a leading AI-law casebook.

## Verified Facts

- The Berkman Klein Center identifies Kaminski as Moses Lasky Professor of Law at the University of Colorado Law School and Director of the Privacy Initiative at Silicon Flatirons.[^1]
- Her work focuses on the law of new technologies, especially information governance, privacy, and freedom of expression; recently AI law, data privacy, and the First Amendment.[^1]
- She is co-authoring an AI-law casebook with Paul Ohm and Andrew Selbst.[^1][^2]
- The authors' casebook site states that *Artificial Intelligence Law* will appear in Foundation Press's University Casebook Series and originated in Ohm's Fall 2023 Georgetown AI & Law course.[^2]
- In 2018 she held a Fulbright-Schuman Innovation Grant on comparative data privacy; in 2024 she researched comparative AI law at the European University Institute as a Fernand Braudel Senior Fellow and Fulbright-Schuman grantee.[^1]
- She previously served as Executive Director of Yale Law School's Information Society Project and is a co-founder of Yale's Media Freedom and Information Access Clinic.[^1]

[^2]: [`SRC-AILA-CASEBOOK`](../../../bibliography.md#SRC-AILA-CASEBOOK) — Author-published casebook site.

## Expert Testimony and Public Advocacy

Kaminski is a doctrinal AI-law scholar rather than a lab-safety engineer. She is useful when the Hugging Face incident is translated into legal categories: automated systems, platform duties, comparative EU AI Act research, and speech/privacy collisions. The forthcoming casebook with Ohm and Selbst is designed for lecture-style AI-law courses grounded in cases, statutes, and rules.

## Relevance to Lawsuits Involving Software and Internet Infrastructure

Kaminski is particularly useful when a matter involves:

- Mapping frontier-AI incidents onto existing U.S. and EU legal frameworks
- Privacy, First Amendment, and automated-decision rules
- Comparative AI regulation (EU AI Act research)

She is a professor, not a default trial-counsel referral. Pair with [Paul Ohm](PERSON-PAUL-OHM.md) on computer crime and with [EVENT-OPENAI-HUGGING-FACE-2026](../legal/EVENT-OPENAI-HUGGING-FACE-2026.md) for the facts of the 2026 intrusion.
