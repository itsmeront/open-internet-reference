# Michael McCaul

## Summary

Michael McCaul is a U.S. Representative from Texas who co-chairs the Congressional Artificial Intelligence Caucus and previously chaired House Homeland Security and Foreign Affairs.

## Verified Facts

- Official House biography states that McCaul is serving his eleventh term representing Texas's 10th District, previously chaired Homeland Security, and in the 118th Congress chaired Foreign Affairs.[^1]
- The same biography states that before Congress he served as Chief of Counter Terrorism and National Security in the U.S. Attorney's office for the Western District of Texas, as Texas Deputy Attorney General, and as a DOJ Public Integrity prosecutor.[^1]
- The Congressional AI Caucus site lists Michael T. McCaul (TX-10) as a co-chair.[^2]
