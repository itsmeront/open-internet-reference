# Don Beyer

## Summary

Don Beyer is a U.S. Representative from Virginia who co-chairs the Congressional Artificial Intelligence Caucus, served on the House AI Task Force, and introduced the AI Foundation Model Transparency Act.

## Verified Facts

- Official House biography states that Beyer represents Virginia's 8th District, serves on Ways and Means and the Joint Economic Committee, was Virginia lieutenant governor (1990–1998), and served as Ambassador to Switzerland and Liechtenstein.[^1]
- The Congressional AI Caucus site lists Donald S. Beyer (VA-08) as a co-chair.[^2]
- Beyer's office describes him as co-chair of the Congressional AI Caucus, a member of the Speaker/Leader AI Task Force, and author of the AI Foundation Model Transparency Act with Reps. Mike Lawler and Sara Jacobs.[^3]
- Obernolte's Task Force page lists Beyer among the 24 members.[^4]

## Historical Context

Beyer is a House AI-caucus institutionalist: transparency and task-force process rather than a development pause. His office also states he has pursued master's coursework in machine learning at George Mason University to inform that work; that claim is self-reported.
