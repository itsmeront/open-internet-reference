# Yoshua Bengio

## Summary

Yoshua Bengio is a full professor at Université de Montréal, founder of Mila – Quebec Artificial Intelligence Institute, and a 2018 A.M. Turing Award laureate known for deep-learning research and for public work on mitigating catastrophic risks from future AI systems.

## Verified Facts

- Mila's directory identifies Bengio as a core academic member, Canada CIFAR AI Chair, and full professor in Université de Montréal's Department of Computer Science and Operations Research.[^1]
- The same profile states that he is most known for pioneering work in deep learning that earned him the 2018 A.M. Turing Award with Geoffrey Hinton and Yann LeCun.[^1]
- He is described as founder and scientific advisor of Mila, senior fellow at CIFAR and co-director of its Learning in Machines & Brains program, and special advisor and founding scientific director of IVADO.[^1]
- Mila states that in 2023 he was appointed to the UN's Scientific Advisory Board for Independent Advice on Breakthroughs in Science and Technology.[^1]
- The profile says he helped draft the Montréal Declaration for the Responsible Development of Artificial Intelligence and continues to raise awareness about mitigating potentially catastrophic risks associated with future AI systems.[^1]


[^1]: [`SRC-BENGIO-MILA`](../../../bibliography.md#SRC-BENGIO-MILA) — Official Mila directory profile.
