# Jay Obernolte

## Summary

Jay Obernolte is a U.S. Representative from California who chairs the House Bipartisan Task Force on Artificial Intelligence, co-chairs the Congressional AI Caucus, and holds an M.S. in artificial intelligence from UCLA.

## Verified Facts

- Official House biography states that Obernolte represents California's 23rd District, serves on Energy and Commerce and as Chairman of the Science Committee's Research and Technology Subcommittee, and holds a B.S. from Caltech, an M.S. in Artificial Intelligence from UCLA, and a Doctorate in Public Administration from California Baptist University.[^1]
- The same biography describes him as a video-game developer and business owner and states he is "currently the only serving member of Congress with a graduate-level degree in AI."[^1]
- The Congressional AI Caucus site lists Jay Obernolte (CA-23) as a co-chair.[^2]
- Obernolte's office states that he chaired the 118th Congress House Bipartisan Task Force on Artificial Intelligence with Co-Chair Ted Lieu and that the Task Force issued a final report after nearly 10 months of work.[^3]

## Historical Context

Obernolte is the House Republican technical lead on AI: caucus co-chair, task-force chair, and a graduate AI degree. The 2024 Task Force report's published framing is U.S. leadership plus guardrails, not a development moratorium.

## Relevance to Open Source and Software Companies

As Research and Technology subcommittee chair and Energy and Commerce member, Obernolte sits at the overlap of NIST standards, FTC-adjacent consumer issues, and federal R&D that open-weight research depends on. The Task Force report is the House counterpart to the Senate AI Working Group roadmap.
