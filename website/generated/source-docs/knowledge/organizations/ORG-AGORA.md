# Agora (human rights lawyers' group)

## Summary

Agora is a Russian human rights lawyers' group associated with Pavel Chikov. OIR includes it because reporting on Telegram's 2018 Russian blocking dispute identifies Chikov as Telegram's lawyer, and institutional sources describe Agora as a lawyers' organization chaired by Chikov.

## Verified Facts

- The Rafto Foundation prize page describes Agora as a countrywide association of human rights organizations and states it consists of lawyers dealing with abuse-of-power cases across most of Russia's regions.[^1]
- The same page identifies Pavel Chikov as having chaired Agora since its founding in 2005.[^1]
- RFE/RL reported that Pavel Chikov represented Telegram in the April 2018 Russian court blocking dispute.[^2]

## Historical Context

OIR treats Agora as a civil-society legal advocacy network rather than a commercial law firm. Later Russian designations, liquidations, or "undesirable organization" status are not yet verified here and are listed as research debt.

[^2]: [`SRC-RFERL-TELEGRAM-BLOCK-2018`](https://www.rferl.org/a/russia-court-orders-telegram-to-be-blocked-in-russia/29164292.html) — RFE/RL — Russian Court Orders Telegram App Blocked (2018). Secondary reporting.

## Analysis

Agora is relevant to OIR as an example of human-rights counsel defending a messaging platform against encryption-access and blocking orders. It should not be confused with U.S. commercial technology law firms also catalogued in OIR.
