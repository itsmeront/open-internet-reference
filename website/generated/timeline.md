## 1984

- **Enactment**: [`STAT-CFAA-1030`](source-docs/knowledge/legal/STAT-CFAA-1030.md) — The Computer Fraud and Abuse Act (CFAA), 18 U.S.C. § 1030, is the primary U.S. federal anti-hacking statute, originally enacted in 1984 and substantially amended in 1986, 1994, 1996, 2001, 2002, and 2008, which criminalizes unauthorized access to computers and has been widely criticized for vagueness and prosecutorial overreach.

## 1996

- **Enactment**: [`STAT-SECTION-230`](source-docs/knowledge/legal/STAT-SECTION-230.md) — Section 230 of the Communications Decency Act (47 U.S.C. § 230), enacted in 1996, provides that internet platforms shall not be treated as publishers of user-generated content and protects good-faith content moderation, forming the legal foundation for the modern internet.
