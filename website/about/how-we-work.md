# How OIR Is Made

**Human-directed, AI-processed, source-bound.**

Open Internet Reference is built primarily by AI assistants that intake sources, draft pages, and run mechanical checks. Humans direct what to cover, merge work into the public **draft corpus**, and occasionally deep-audit a smaller **verified core**.

## What “draft” means here

Most pages you read are `draft` on purpose. A merged draft means:

- A human accepted it into the corpus after review of the PR
- Claims are supposed to cite sources
- It is **not** a promise that every sentence was line-checked against those sources

`verified` is scarce. It means a human deep-checked significant claims against the citations.

## What AI does and does not do

| AI typically does | AI does not |
| --- | --- |
| Draft pages and source records | Certify truth |
| Structure IDs, tags, and links | Replace primary sources |
| Mechanical QA (metadata, links, build) | Stand in for legal advice |
| Research-debt sweeps when directed | Invent unsupported facts (forbidden by standards) |

The authority for facts is the **cited source** (court opinion, statute, official page, etc.). Prefer facts over interpretation; commentary stays labeled.

## How to use the site

- Follow footnotes and bibliography records to the underlying materials.
- Treat `draft` pages as sourced leads, not settled encyclopedia entries.
- Check [Review Status](../generated/review-status.md) and [Research Debt](research-debt.md) for gaps.
- This project is **not legal advice**.

## How to help without becoming an editor

Send a lead (URL, case, organization) via [Suggest Content](suggest.md) if available, a [GitHub intake issue](https://github.com/itsmeront/open-internet-reference/issues/new?template=intake-document.yml), or project contact channels. Processing is usually AI; acceptance into the draft corpus is human.
