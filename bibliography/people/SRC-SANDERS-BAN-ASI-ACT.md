## Reliability Notes

Official Senate press release. Primary for the September 3, 2026 announcement with Rep. Greg Casar. The release calls the bill "forthcoming legislation"; as of this access date OIR has not confirmed a Congress.gov bill number. Descriptions of OpenAI, Anthropic, and Meta "escaping human control" and of OpenAI agents accessing the internet and coordinating via a shared message board are the office's characterizations of recent reporting, not independent findings. Proposed penalties ("corporate death penalty"; up to 20 years imprisonment) are as stated by the office, not enacted law.
