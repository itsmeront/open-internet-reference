## Reliability Notes

Closest thing to an independent technical account. Authors state they took no payment from OpenAI, worked on premises, and that OpenAI could redact non-public information from the post. Scope was limited (investigation period June 26–July 13, with analysis focused on July 7–13; earlier training-time message-board activity and later OpenAI-infrastructure compromise were out of scope). Use for agent counts, message-board activity, and collaboration findings. Do not treat as a complete forensic of OpenAI's internal response.
