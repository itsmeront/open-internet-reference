## Reliability Notes

Official House page. Primary for the Task Force's charter (Speaker Johnson and Leader Jeffries each appointed twelve members), co-chairs Jay Obernolte (CA-23) and Ted Lieu (CA-36), the 24-member roster, and the statement that the Task Force produced a final report after nearly 10 months of work. The report PDF is published at https://www.speaker.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/AI-Task-Force-Report-FINAL.pdf. This was a 118th Congress Speaker/Leader task force, not a standing caucus. The report's framing is U.S. AI leadership with "guardrails," not a development moratorium.
