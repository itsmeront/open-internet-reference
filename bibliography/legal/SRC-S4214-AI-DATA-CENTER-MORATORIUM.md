## Reliability Notes

Primary legislative record. Congress.gov lists Sen. Bernard Sanders (I-VT) as sponsor, introduced 2026-03-25, referred to the Senate Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation. Introduced text states the short title "Artificial Intelligence Data Center Moratorium Act" and would, beginning on enactment, bar construction or upgrading of new or existing artificial intelligence data centers until specified federal safeguards are enacted. Status as of access was Introduced. This is proposed legislation, not enacted law.

GPO introduced text is also published at https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/s4214/BILLS-119s4214is.htm.
