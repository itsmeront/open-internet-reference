## Reliability Notes

Primary legislative record. Congress.gov lists Sen. Josh Hawley as sponsor, Sen. Richard Blumenthal as original cosponsor, introduced 2025-09-29, referred to Senate Commerce, Science, and Transportation. Short title is the Artificial Intelligence Risk Evaluation Act of 2025. Introduced text would require the Secretary of Energy to establish an Advanced Artificial Intelligence Evaluation Program. Proposed legislation, not enacted law.
