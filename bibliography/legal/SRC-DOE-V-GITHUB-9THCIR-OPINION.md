## Reliability Notes

Official published opinion, No. 24-7700, D.C. No. 4:22-cv-06823-JST. Argued and submitted February 11, 2026; filed September 16, 2026. Panel: Judges Sidney R. Thomas and Eric D. Miller, and District Judge Stanley Blumenfeld, Jr., sitting by designation. The opinion affirms dismissal of the DMCA § 1202(b) output theory and treats the input theory as forfeited.
