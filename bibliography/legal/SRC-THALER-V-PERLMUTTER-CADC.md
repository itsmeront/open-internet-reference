## Reliability Notes

RECAP copy of the D.C. Circuit opinion in No. 23-5233. Primary appellate authority for the statutory human-authorship requirement as applied to a work the applicant identified as autonomously generated by AI. The court did not decide whether the Constitution independently requires human authorship, and it treated as waived Thaler’s later argument that he was the human author by directing the system.
