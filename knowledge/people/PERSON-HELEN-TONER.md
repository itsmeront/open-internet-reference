# Helen Toner

## Summary

Helen Toner is Executive Director of Georgetown's Center for Security and Emerging Technology, an AI-governance researcher who has advised policymakers on AI strategy and testified before congressional committees.

## Verified Facts

- CSET's staff profile identifies Toner as Executive Director at Georgetown's Center for Security and Emerging Technology.[^1]
- She previously worked as a Senior Research Analyst at Open Philanthropy, advising policymakers and grantmakers on AI policy and strategy.[^1]
- Between Open Philanthropy and CSET, she lived in Beijing studying the Chinese AI ecosystem as a Research Affiliate of Oxford University's Center for the Governance of AI.[^1]
- CSET states that she has written for *Foreign Affairs*, *The Economist*, *TIME*, and other outlets about U.S.–China competition and AI policy, and that she has testified before several Congressional committees.[^1]
- She holds an M.A. in Security Studies from Georgetown and a B.Sc. in Chemical Engineering and a Diploma in Languages from the University of Melbourne.[^1]
