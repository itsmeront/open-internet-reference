# Mike Rounds

## Summary

Mike Rounds is a U.S. Senator from South Dakota who co-chairs the Senate Artificial Intelligence Caucus and served on the 2024 Bipartisan Senate AI Working Group that released a legislative roadmap.

## Verified Facts

- Official Senate biography states that Rounds was sworn into the Senate on January 6, 2015, previously served as South Dakota's 31st governor (2003–2011) and in the state senate (1991–2000), and lists Appropriations, Armed Services, Banking, Indian Affairs, and Intelligence among current committees.[^1]
- Heinrich's Senate AI Caucus page lists Rounds as co-chair, succeeding former Sen. Rob Portman.[^2]
- The May 15, 2024 Working Group announcement names Rounds as a member of the four-senator group that released the Senate AI roadmap after nine Insight Forums.[^3]

## Historical Context

Rounds is the Republican co-chair of the standing Senate AI forum and was one of four senators who ran the 2023–2024 Insight Forums. That work is bipartisan innovation-and-guardrails policy, not a pause-and-ban program.

## Relevance to Open Source and Software Companies

As Intelligence and Armed Services member plus AI caucus co-chair, Rounds is a primary Senate contact for national-security framing of open-weight models, export controls, and evaluation incidents.
