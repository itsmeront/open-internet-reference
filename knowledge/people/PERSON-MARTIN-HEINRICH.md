# Martin Heinrich

## Summary

Martin Heinrich is a U.S. Senator from New Mexico who co-founded the Senate Artificial Intelligence Caucus in 2019 and served on the 2024 Bipartisan Senate AI Working Group that released a legislative roadmap.

## Verified Facts

- Official Senate biography states that Heinrich has a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Missouri, served two House terms, has been a U.S. Senator since 2012, and is Ranking Member of the Committee on Energy and Natural Resources.[^1]
- Heinrich's caucus page states that he and Sen. Rob Portman formed the bipartisan Senate Artificial Intelligence Caucus in 2019 and that he remains a co-chair with Sen. Mike Rounds.[^2]
- On May 15, 2024, Heinrich's office announced that he, Majority Leader Chuck Schumer, Todd Young, and Mike Rounds had released a Senate AI policy roadmap after nine AI Insight Forums.[^3]

## Historical Context

Heinrich's AI work is the Senate's longest-running bipartisan caucus track: national AI initiative provisions, government-use rules, and research-resource access rather than a development pause. The 2024 roadmap sits in that "innovation plus guardrails" lane, distinct from the 2026 Sanders/Casar pause-and-ban bills.

## Relevance to Open Source and Software Companies

Caucus-associated research-infrastructure bills (NAIRR / CREATE AI as described on Heinrich's pages) would expand shared compute and data access that open and academic labs use. The roadmap's IP, privacy, and safety headings remain the Senate's most comprehensive bipartisan catalog of where committees already claimed consensus.
