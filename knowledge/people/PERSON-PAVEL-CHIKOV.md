# Pavel Chikov

## Summary

Pavel Chikov is a Russian human rights lawyer identified in 2018 reporting as counsel representing Telegram against Russian blocking efforts, and described by the Rafto Foundation as chair of Agora, a human rights lawyers' organization.

## Verified Facts

- RFE/RL reported that Pavel Chikov, described as a prominent human rights activist and lawyer who represents Telegram, said the April 2018 Moscow court ruling ordering Telegram blocked was unlawful and unfounded.[^1]
- The Rafto Foundation prize page describes Pavel Chikov as chair of Agora and states Agora consists of lawyers dealing with abuse-of-power cases across most of Russia's regions.[^2]
