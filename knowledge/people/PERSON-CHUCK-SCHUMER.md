# Chuck Schumer

## Summary

Chuck Schumer is a U.S. Senator from New York who led the Bipartisan Senate AI Working Group with Rounds, Heinrich, and Young and released a May 2024 Senate AI policy roadmap after nine Insight Forums.

## Verified Facts

- Official Senate biography states that Schumer was first elected to the Senate in 1998, was elected Democratic leader in 2016, became Senate Majority Leader in 2021, and was re-elected in 2022 to a fifth term.[^1]
- The May 15, 2024 Working Group announcement names Majority Leader Charles Schumer as a member of the four-senator group that released a Senate AI policy roadmap after months of discussion, hundreds of stakeholder meetings, and nine all-Senator AI Insight Forums.[^2]

## Historical Context

Schumer's 2023 SAFE Innovation framing and Insight Forums were the Senate leadership vehicle for AI. The resulting roadmap is a committee to-do list, not a pause statute. Later 2026 pause-and-ban bills from Sanders sit outside that leadership product.

## Relevance to Open Source and Software Companies

The Insight Forums included open-ecosystem participants (the roadmap PDF lists Hugging Face CEO Clément Delangue among invited stakeholders). The roadmap's copyright, privacy, and research-cloud headings remain the starting map for how Senate committees talk about training data and shared compute.
