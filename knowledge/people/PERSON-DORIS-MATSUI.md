# Doris Matsui

## Summary

Doris Matsui is a U.S. Representative from California who co-chairs the Congressional Artificial Intelligence Caucus and is Ranking Member of the House Energy and Commerce Communications and Technology Subcommittee.

## Verified Facts

- Official House biography states that Matsui has represented Sacramento and surrounding areas since 2005, is a senior member of Energy and Commerce, and serves as Ranking Member of the Communications and Technology Subcommittee.[^1]
- The same biography states that she served on President Clinton's transition board and later as Deputy Assistant to the President in the White House Office of Public Liaison, and that she succeeded her late husband Robert Matsui in a March 2005 special election.[^1]
- The Congressional AI Caucus site lists Doris O. Matsui (CA-07) as a co-chair.[^2]
